About

I am a postdoctoral scholar at the Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics. I received my Ph.D. in theoretical physics from Cornell University, where I studied under Erich Mueller. My undergraduate degree is in Physics and Italian from Georgetown University, where I rowed on the lightweight crew team.

Outside of physics, I enjoy cooking, traveling, hiking, rock climbing and playing tennis.
