Research

I work on problems in strongly correlated quantum matter and quantum dynamics, with a focus on near-term applications in atomic, molecular and optical (AMO) experiments and other analog quantum simulators such as transmon qubits. My graduate work addressed equilibrium phases and transport in low-dimensional quantum matter, where quantum fluctuations and strong interactions compete. As a postdoc I have studied out-of-equilibrium states generated by open and monitored quantum dynamics, which matter more and more as quantum computers move toward error correction. I am also interested in better numerical methods for classical simulation of quantum states.
